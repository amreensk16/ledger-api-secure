== Even with Istio mTLS/AuthorizationPolicy active, NetworkPolicy independently blocks ==
== an unrelated namespace's plaintext attempt at the network layer (L3/L4), before it ==
== would ever reach Envoy's identity check ==

curl from an unrelated pod in the default namespace:
curl: (28) Connection timed out after 3001 milliseconds
HTTP_CODE=000

This demonstrates the two layers are complementary, not redundant:
- NetworkPolicy (kernel/netfilter, L3/L4): blocks based on IP/namespace/port -
  catches raw traffic and works even if Envoy itself were bypassed or buggy.
- Istio mTLS + AuthorizationPolicy (userspace sidecar, L7, SPIFFE identity):
  catches identity spoofing NetworkPolicy cannot - a workload can't fake
  ledger-api's or reporting's SPIFFE identity without its private key, and
  authorization is enforced on WHO is calling, not merely WHERE from.
